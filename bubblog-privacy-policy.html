English

Overview

Bubblog is a time-tracking application that helps you visualize how you spend your day using colorful bubbles. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

  • Time-tracking data - Categories, durations, and notes you enter are stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • No advertising data - Bubblog does not use advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob) and does not display ads.
  • In-App Purchase - Premium purchases are processed exclusively by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.
  • Crash / diagnostic data - Anonymized crash reports may be sent to Apple if you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in your iOS settings.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar information
  • Device identifiers for advertising

Third-Party Services

Bubblog does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or social platforms. The only third-party service is Apple App Store for In-App Purchases.

Data Retention and Deletion

All your data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Bubblog is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
